⚖️ The 3 Unbreakable EA-AI Rules

🧠
AI drafts. You decide.

Gemini produces structure and language. You decide what gets sent, approved, or escalated. Never bypass your own judgement.

🔍
Verify every fact.

Names, amounts, dates, approvals — always cross-check against the original source. Gemini can hallucinate. You cannot afford to.

🔒
Protect confidentiality.

Only share information that is permitted by company policy. Never paste sensitive personal, financial, or strategic data into AI unless authorized.

✅ What Gemini does brilliantly

  • Summarize long emails and threads
  • Draft professional replies in seconds
  • Structure MOM from rough notes
  • Extract action items with owners
  • Rewrite unclear messages with better tone
  • Create agendas, briefs, and trackers
  • Prioritize tasks using logic you provide
  • Format any data into tables or reports

⚠️ What Gemini cannot replace

  • Reading the room in a meeting
  • Knowing which executive is sensitive about what
  • Making an approval decision
  • Verifying whether a vendor is trustworthy
  • Knowing unwritten company rules
  • Handling a crisis with human empathy
  • Taking responsibility for a sent message
  • Protecting relationships — that is always you

Gemini & Skills — Your AI Toolkit

Gemini is not just a chatbot. When you understand its layers — chat, Skills, and Gems — you unlock a system that handles recurring EA work automatically, consistently, and fast.

🧱 The 3 Layers of Gemini for EAs

💬
Layer 1 — Chat
One-off tasks

Open Gemini and type or paste. Best for quick drafts, instant summaries, or ad-hoc questions. No setup required.

Use when: A one-time, unpredictable task arrives
⚙️
Layer 2 — Skills
Standing instructions

Saved instructions that Gemini applies automatically when you do a matching task. Created once, used forever. Works across Google Workspace.

Use when: A task repeats weekly — MOM, email triage, reports
💎
Layer 3 — Gems
Specialist agents

Focused AI assistants you build and name. Each Gem is pre-loaded with a role, rules, and a workflow. One click to activate the right expert.

Use when: A complex EA job needs consistent, high-quality output

⚙️ What are Gemini Skills?

Skills are standing instructions stored in Gemini that automatically apply when you're doing a matching task in Gmail, Docs, or Workspace. Think of them as your personal EA rules that Gemini has memorized.

📌 Example: Email Skill
Instruction saved: "Always classify emails as URGENT/ACTION/WAITING/INFO and extract action items with owners."

Result: Every time you open an email in Gmail and ask Gemini for help, it applies this rule automatically.
📌 Example: MOM Skill
Instruction saved: "Always structure meeting outputs with: Objective, Key Points, Decisions, Actions, Owner, Deadline, Open Questions."

Result: Paste notes into Docs, ask Gemini to structure it — it follows your MOM format every time.
